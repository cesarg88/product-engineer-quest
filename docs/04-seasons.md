# Seasons

## Purpose

A Season is a focused learning cycle inside PEQ.

The goal of a Season is to avoid vague long-term growth plans and replace them with a clear short-term mission.

A Season should be long enough to create meaningful progress, but short enough to maintain motivation.

---

## Duration

A PEQ Season lasts 12 weeks.

This duration is intentionally similar to a professional quarter.

It gives the player enough time to practice, produce evidence, review progress, and adapt the next cycle.

---

## Season Structure

Each Season defines:

- a theme;
- a main goal;
- the primary skill focus;
- the secondary skill focus;
- the expected evidence;
- the progress target;
- the definition of success.

The structure should remain lightweight.

If defining a Season becomes harder than following it, the system is too complex.

## Starting a Season

Before starting a Season, define:

1. What professional evolution is being trained?
2. Which skill receives the most attention?
3. What kind of evidence should exist at the end?
4. What is the minimum viable weekly rhythm?
5. What would make the Season feel successful?

A Season should not start with a large backlog.

It should start with a small first week.

---

## Weekly Rhythm

Each week should contain a small number of quests.

Recommended weekly rhythm for PEQ v0.1:

- one Study Quest or Analysis Quest;
- one visible deliverable;
- one short Weekly Review.

Optional Writing Quests can be added when there is enough energy and context.

The weekly rhythm should be sustainable even during busy weeks.

---

## Completing a Season

A Season is complete when there is enough evidence that the player can think, communicate, or decide differently than before.

Completion should not be based only on points.

Points show progress, but evidence shows growth.

---

## Season Review

At the end of a Season, review:

1. What evidence was produced?
2. Which skill improved the most?
3. What felt useful?
4. What felt like friction?
5. What should be removed?
6. What should continue?
7. What should the next Season focus on?

The next Season should only be designed after this review.

---

## Core Rule

A Season should create focus, not pressure.

If the Season starts to feel like a rigid course, simplify it.
