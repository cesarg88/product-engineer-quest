# Season 1: From Builder to Product Thinker

## Theme

From Builder to Product Thinker.

Season 1 focuses on the first professional shift PEQ is designed to support:

> Stop seeing software only as implementation work and start seeing it as a product decision with user, business, and technical consequences.

---

## Main Goal

By the end of Season 1, the player should be able to look at a feature and reason clearly about:

- the user problem;
- the product hypothesis;
- the success metric;
- the simplest useful version;
- the main trade-offs;
- the engineering role in shaping the outcome.

---

## Duration

12 weeks.

---

## Primary Skill Focus

Product Thinking.

---

## Secondary Skill Focus

Product Leadership.

AI Engineering and Business Thinking are included, but with lighter emphasis.

---

## Progress Target

Season 1 target:

**100 points**

Suggested scoring:

- Study Quest: 5 points
- Analysis Quest: 10 points
- Writing Quest: 15 points
- Weekly Review: 5 points

The target exists to make progress visible.

It should not encourage shallow completion.

---

## Minimum Weekly Rhythm

Each week should aim for:

1. One small quest.
2. One visible deliverable.
3. One short Weekly Review.

A good week is not a perfect week.

A good week is a week where something was practiced and captured.

---

## Recommended Evidence

By the end of Season 1, useful evidence may include:

- product analyses;
- short hypotheses;
- metric definitions;
- small RFCs;
- trade-off notes;
- AI-assisted product thinking exercises;
- weekly reviews;
- before/after examples of improved reasoning.

There is no fixed number of documents required.

The quality of reasoning matters more than volume.

---

## Example Week

A simple Season 1 week could look like this:

1. Read one chapter, article, or product case.
2. Analyze one feature from Fever, PickOne, Qonto, Revolut, or another product.
3. Write a short note answering:
   - What problem does this solve?
   - What hypothesis is behind it?
   - How would success be measured?
   - What would be the simplest useful version?
4. Complete the Weekly Review.

---

## Definition of Success

Season 1 is successful if the player can demonstrate a visible shift in reasoning.

Before Season 1:

> How should I implement this?

After Season 1:

> Why are we building this, who is it for, how will we know if it worked, and what is the simplest useful version we can deliver safely?

---

## What Season 1 Is Not

Season 1 is not about:

- becoming a Product Manager;
- mastering business strategy;
- creating a complex website;
- building a complete RPG system;
- reading as much as possible;
- producing many documents with shallow reasoning.

Season 1 is about practicing product-oriented judgment.

---

## First Milestone

The first milestone is intentionally small:

> Complete one product analysis and one Weekly Review.

Once this milestone is done, the system has started.

---

## Core Rule

Season 1 should stay simple enough to follow during a normal working week.
