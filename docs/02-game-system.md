# Game System

## Purpose

The PEQ game system exists to make professional growth visible and easy to follow.

It should stay simple.

The system must help the player answer four questions:

1. What am I training?
2. What should I do this week?
3. What evidence will I produce?
4. How do I know I made progress?

If a mechanic adds complexity without improving clarity, motivation, or evidence, it should be removed.

---

## Season

PEQ is organized into Seasons.

A Season lasts 12 weeks and focuses on a specific professional evolution.

Current Season:

**Season 1: From Builder to Product Thinker**

The goal of Season 1 is to help a Senior iOS Engineer start reasoning more clearly about users, product decisions, business context, trade-offs, and measurable impact.

---

## Skills

PEQ v0.1 focuses on four skills:

1. Product Thinking
2. Business Thinking
3. Product Leadership
4. AI Engineering

These skills are not unlocked through theory.

They improve through practice, evidence, and reflection.

---

## Quests

A Quest is a small professional practice task.

Each Quest must produce evidence.

PEQ v0.1 uses only three quest types.

### Study Quest

Learn from a selected source.

Examples:

- read a chapter;
- study an article;
- watch a product talk.

Output:

- short summary;
- key takeaway;
- application to real work, PickOne, or PEQ.

Reward:

**5 points**

---

### Analysis Quest

Analyze a product, feature, user flow, or decision.

Output:

- user problem;
- hypothesis;
- metric;
- trade-offs;
- possible improvement.

Reward:

**10 points**

---

### Writing Quest

Create a short professional artifact.

Examples:

- RFC;
- product brief;
- decision note;
- trade-off analysis.

Output:

- structured document;
- clear recommendation;
- reasoning behind the recommendation.

Reward:

**15 points**

---

## Weekly Review

Every week ends with a short review.

The review answers:

1. What did I complete?
2. What did I learn?
3. What evidence did I produce?
4. What was difficult?
5. What will I do next week?

Reward:

**5 points**

The weekly review should take no more than 10 minutes.

---

## Progress

Season 1 has a target of:

**100 points**

Points are used only to make progress visible.

They are not the real goal.

The real goal is better professional judgment.

---

## Anti-Abandonment Rules

1. Keep quests small.
2. Always know the next action.
3. Produce something visible every week.
4. Do not punish missed weeks.
5. Avoid perfectionism.
6. Remove any mechanic that creates friction.

---

## Core Rule

PEQ should be useful before it becomes complete.
