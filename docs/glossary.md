# Glossary

## PEQ

The Product Engineer Quest.

A structured, RPG-inspired professional development system designed to help software engineers evolve into Product Engineers.

---

## Product Engineer

An engineer who combines strong technical execution with product judgment.

A Product Engineer helps teams understand user problems, reason about trade-offs, define success, and build useful solutions.

---

## Season

A focused 12-week learning cycle.

Each Season has a theme, a main goal, a skill focus, expected evidence, and a review at the end.

---

## Quest

A small practice task inside PEQ.

Each Quest should produce evidence, such as a note, analysis, hypothesis, RFC, or review.

---

## Study Quest

A Quest focused on learning from a selected source.

Examples include reading a chapter, studying an article, or watching a product talk.

---

## Analysis Quest

A Quest focused on analyzing a product, feature, flow, or decision.

It should usually identify the user problem, hypothesis, metric, trade-offs, and possible improvement.

---

## Writing Quest

A Quest focused on producing a professional artifact.

Examples include an RFC, product brief, decision note, or trade-off analysis.

---

## Weekly Review

A short weekly reflection used to capture progress, learning, evidence, friction, and the next action.

The Weekly Review should remain lightweight.

---

## Evidence

Concrete proof of learning or improved reasoning.

Evidence may include product analyses, hypotheses, RFCs, trade-off notes, AI workflows, weekly reviews, or real work applications.

---

## Product Thinking

The ability to reason about software through user problems, hypotheses, metrics, MVPs, and trade-offs.

---

## Business Thinking

The ability to understand how product and engineering decisions affect business outcomes, costs, risks, and opportunities.

---

## Product Leadership

The ability to create clarity, influence decisions, communicate trade-offs, and improve outcomes without relying on formal authority.

---

## AI Engineering

The ability to use AI as a professional multiplier while keeping human judgment in control.

---

## Points

A simple progress mechanism used in PEQ v0.1.

Points make progress visible but are not the real goal.

The real goal is better professional judgment.

---

## Definition of Success

A clear description of what should be different at the end of a Season.

For PEQ, success should be based on demonstrated reasoning and evidence, not only task completion.
