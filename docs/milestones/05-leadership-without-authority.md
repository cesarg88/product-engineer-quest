# Milestone 5 — Leadership Without Authority

## Objective

Learn to influence product and technical decisions without relying on formal authority.

## Competencies Trained

Primary:

- Leadership Without Authority

Secondary:

- Product Thinking
- Business Thinking

## Why This Matters

Senior and Product Engineers often create impact by asking better questions, clarifying trade-offs, and helping teams converge on decisions.

## Tools Needed

- ChatGPT as PM, designer, backend engineer, CTO, or interviewer
- Real or simulated product scenarios
- Written responses

## Exercises

Simulate 5 professional conversations:

1. PM proposes a feature with unclear value.
2. Designer proposes a visually strong but complex solution.
3. Backend says a solution will take longer than expected.
4. Stakeholder asks for “just a small change”.
5. QA finds an edge case close to release.

For each scenario, write a response that:

- acknowledges the concern;
- clarifies the goal;
- explains the trade-off;
- proposes a next step.

## Completion Criteria

This milestone is complete when the player can respond productively without sounding defensive, overly technical, or passive.

## Feedback Loop

Each response receives feedback on tone, clarity, influence, and decision quality.
